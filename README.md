# Pause

**Pause helps people spot suspicious links and messages before they click, pay, reply, or share sensitive information.**

Scams work because they create pressure: a supposed traffic offence, an account problem, a delivery waiting, a reward about to expire. They imitate trusted organisations, use convincing web addresses, and make people feel that they must act immediately.

Pause creates a moment to stop and inspect.

## What Pause does

Someone can paste a link or message into Pause, share it from another app, or use a screenshot. Pause examines the warning signs and explains them in plain language.

It looks for things such as:

- A web address that resembles a real organisation but uses the wrong domain.
- Lookalike characters, including Unicode homograph attacks.
- Hidden destinations, redirects, shortened links, and unusual URL formatting.
- Known phishing or malicious links.
- Urgency, threats, impersonation, and requests for money, passwords, banking details, or one-time codes.

When Pause recognises an impersonated organisation, it points the person to the verified official channel instead of asking them to investigate the suspicious link.

## Why it matters

In September 2026, the Federal Road Safety Corps warned Nigerians about scam messages that claimed a new road-traffic offence had been recorded. The messages pointed people to `frscgov.top/ng`, a deceptive site with no affiliation to FRSC. The official website is `frsc.gov.ng`.

The danger was not only the link. The message used urgency and trusted public-service branding to push people into acting before they had time to verify it.

That is the moment Pause is built for.

## How Pause communicates risk

Pause does not label a link “safe.” It gives people the evidence they need to make a safer decision:

- **High risk** — strong signs of phishing, impersonation, credential theft, or known malicious activity.
- **Caution** — meaningful warning signs that should be independently verified.
- **Unable to verify** — not enough reliable information to make a judgement.
- **No known warning found** — no warning was detected; this is never a guarantee of safety.

Every result includes a practical next step: do not open the link, verify through an official website, contact the organisation through a verified channel, or preserve the message as evidence.

## Privacy and trust

Pause is designed to protect people without treating their private messages as a product.

- It analyses only content a person deliberately chooses to check.
- It does not open suspicious links merely to inspect them.
- It minimises and redacts sensitive information before any remote analysis.
- It does not promise universal access to messages, email, or notifications.
- It does not sell, advertise against, or train on private communications.

## Our principle

> Stop. Inspect. Verify.

Pause is a safety companion—not a replacement for careful judgement or official support channels.
